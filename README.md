# iRespond

**See it. Own it. Solve it. Prove the impact.**

iRespond is a mobile-first global Community Action Network that turns visible community needs into verified, planned, resourced, executed, measured, maintained, and replicable action.

## Core doctrine

- **Response to Ability:** people contribute according to their available time, skills, resources, relationships, influence, access, care, or funding.
- **3P Action Compass:** Place, Position, and Possessions/Capabilities.
- **Community ownership:** affected communities participate in defining, contributing to, validating, and sustaining solutions.
- **Verified impact:** the platform follows action from report through verification, delivery, outcomes, maintenance, and replication.
- **Mobile first:** camera, location, evidence capture, offline operation, low-bandwidth use, notifications, and fast contribution flows are first-class product capabilities.
- **SDG aligned:** the platform directly supports more than half of the UN Sustainable Development Goals and is designed to map projects across all 17.

## Initial product surfaces

- iOS and Android mobile applications
- Responsive public/institutional web
- API and event platform
- Trust, verification, safeguarding, and fraud operations
- Project and contribution orchestration
- Impact Passport and verifiable contribution history

## Initial engineering domains

1. Identity and trust
2. Communities and geospatial discovery
3. Need reporting and evidence
4. Verification and community challenge
5. Project conversion and project rooms
6. Ability profiles, volunteering, and non-cash contribution matching
7. Funding orchestration through licensed payment providers
8. Impact evidence, outcomes, and maintenance
9. Reputation and credentials
10. Notifications, search, analytics, and AI assistance

## Delivery policy

Changes should be developed through short-lived branches and pull requests. Consequential workflows require tests, explicit authorization, auditability, and safe failure behaviour.
