FIELDWORK is a new design language for iRespond: warmer, more human, more visually distinctive, and designed to make community action easier to understand and complete. It keeps the iRespond brand and its core mission.
Editorial storytelling meets trustworthy, functional software.

The prototype includes responsive layouts, original artwork, functional search and opportunity filters, sample-detail interactions, a theme switch, and a typography and color gallery. Example needs are explicitly labeled as demonstration data.
These are captures of the implemented responsive web interface. The native iOS and Android versions still require device-level visual testing.
The previous design was dominated by dark panels and conventional dashboard cards. FIELDWORK introduces a warmer canvas, a more distinctive identity, original illustration, expressive yet readable typography, and a simpler decision hierarchy.
Functionally, the design separates discovering needs, reporting observations, contributing abilities and reviewing verified activity. It makes trust states more understandable, preserves offline recovery and keeps the institutional workflows distinct from the consumer experience.
The inspiration combines the accessible volunteer discovery of [COMON](https://www.behance.net/gallery/252753177/COMON-Case-Study-App-UXUI-Design?utm_source=chatgpt.com), proximity-based opportunities in [VoluNear](https://www.behance.net/gallery/245594757/VoluNear-Volunteering-Mobile-App-UIUX-Case-Study?utm_source=chatgpt.com), transparent progress presentation in [Fundio](https://www.behance.net/gallery/248432207/Donation-Mobile-App-UX-UI-Case-Study?utm_source=chatgpt.com), and editorial nonprofit design directions on [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/27330556-Nonprofit-Website-Design?utm_source=chatgpt.com). The resulting design and illustration are original.
